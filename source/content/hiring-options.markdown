# Ways to work together

Every collaboration starts the same way: a short conversation to understand what
you're trying to achieve and whether I'm the right person for it. From there, we
pick a shape that fits.

## By project

Best for a defined outcome — a new product, a migration, an architectural
change. We look at the problem together, agree on the requirements and a plan,
and I'll give you an honest estimate of value, cost and timing. Deployment is
rarely the finish line, so the result is built to keep evolving.

## By fixed period

Best when you already have a team and a running product. I embed for an agreed
period — often a few days a month, or a short full-time sprint — and work in
hourly units at a predefined rate. You get flexibility; I get to go deep.

## As a fractional CTO

Best for early-stage teams without a technical co-founder. A continuous,
part-time commitment to your technical direction: strategy, hiring, process and
architecture, with hands-on delivery when it counts.
